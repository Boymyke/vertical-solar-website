Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Vertical Energy & Networks Ltd ("Vertical Solar", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through verticalsolar.com.ng and our services, why, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

What we collect

  • Details you give us — name, phone, email, address and information about your power needs when you request a quote, contact us, open a support ticket, apply for financing or place an order.
  • Chat conversations — messages you send to our AI assistant, including voice messages converted to text. Our team can review conversations to improve service and follow up on enquiries.
  • Usage analytics — pages visited, time on page, scroll depth, clicks, device type, browser and approximate city and country. This is counted on every visit to keep the website working well. It uses no cookies, we do not store your IP address, and it cannot identify you personally. Google Analytics is only used if you allow it.
  • Payment information — online payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card details.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes and deliver installations
  • To provide support, maintenance reminders and warranty service
  • To improve our website and services
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

Our legal bases are your consent, performance of a contract with you, and our legitimate interest in running and improving our business.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (hosting, email delivery, payment processing, AI processing), under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

Retention

We keep customer records for as long as you are a customer and for the period required for warranties, tax and legal purposes. Analytics data is kept for up to 180 days in detailed form.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. To turn off optional Google Analytics on this device, click below. To make any other request, email Verticalenergy.ng@gmail.com.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, password hashing and audit logs to protect your data.

Contact

Vertical Energy & Networks Ltd, 5 Agbonyin Street, Off Nnobi Street, Surulere, Lagos. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
