Quick checks

Inverter is beeping
Switch off heavy appliances (AC, iron, pump, microwave). If the beeping stops, the system was overloaded. If it continues, note the error code on the screen and open a ticket. What the alarms mean.
Batteries run out early at night
Check whether panels are dusty and whether new appliances were added. On cloudy days, enable grid or generator charging. If this happens every night, your battery may need a health check.
No power from the inverter
Check that the inverter is switched on, the battery breaker is on, and no breaker in the distribution board has tripped. Reset once only — if it trips again, stop and call us.
Panels not charging
Look at the PV reading on the inverter around midday. If it's zero on a sunny day, check the DC isolator near the inverter is on. Do not touch panel cables.
Smoke, burning smell, sparks or swollen batteries: switch off the inverter and battery breaker, keep everyone away, and call us now.
