We're energy engineers who happen to love solar

Vertical Energy & Networks Ltd was founded in 2017 through a partnership with GreenStreak Energies Limited (GSEL). Today, as Vertical Solar, we design, install and manage solar, hybrid and industrial power systems from Lagos to Kano.

What we believe

Most solar problems in Nigeria are not caused by the sun. They come from systems sold without a load audit, batteries too small for the evening, cables too thin for the current, and no one to call when something beeps.

So we work differently. An engineer sizes every system from the loads you actually run. Our own crews install it to a written standard. And our CRM keeps track of your warranties and reminds us when your next maintenance visit is due.

Engineering-led design
