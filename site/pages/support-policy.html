Support policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

How to reach support

Open a ticket at /support, call 08186311330 or message us on WhatsApp. Every request receives a ticket number.

Response times

  • Safety issues (smoke, burning smell, swollen batteries): call immediately; we prioritise these.
  • System down: we aim to respond the same working day.
  • General questions: within one working day.

Maintenance

We recommend a professional maintenance visit every six months. Our system schedules and reminds you of visits after installation.

Warranty claims

Provide your installation reference, a description of the issue and photos where possible. We diagnose the fault and, where covered, manage the claim with the manufacturer.

Chargeable visits

Visits for issues not covered by warranty, or outside our branch cities, may attract a call-out fee that we tell you about in advance.
