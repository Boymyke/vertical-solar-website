Accessibility statement

Last updated: 25 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use our website, including people who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, have motor or cognitive disabilities, or use assistive technology.

What we have built in

  • An accessibility panel (the round button at the bottom left, or press Alt + A) to enlarge text, increase contrast, switch to a highly readable font, add text spacing, highlight links, pause animations, enlarge the cursor, show a reading guide and use grayscale.
  • Read aloud for pages and articles, and voice input and voice replies in our chat assistant.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus, and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Semantic headings, landmarks and labelled forms for screen readers; descriptive alternative text on images.
  • Respect for your device's reduced-motion setting.
  • Content in English, Nigerian Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa and Yorùbá.

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.
