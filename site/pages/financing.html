How PaaP works

  1. Choose your system

    Pick a package or get a custom design from a free load audit.

  2. Apply

    Share basic details and proof of income or business turnover. We review quickly.

  3. Pay the deposit

    Once approved, pay the agreed deposit and we schedule installation.

  4. Enjoy power, pay monthly

    Pay instalments by transfer or direct debit. Maintenance stays included during the plan.

Financing questions

How much is the deposit?
The deposit depends on the system size and term. Your finance officer will give you exact figures after reviewing your application.
What happens if I pay off early?
You can settle the balance at any time. Ask us about early settlement terms when you apply.
Is maintenance included?
Yes, scheduled maintenance visits are included while your plan is active on complete systems.
Can businesses apply?
Yes. For businesses we look at turnover and current energy spend, and can structure payments around your fuel savings.
