From first call to first sunrise

Most home systems are installed within a week of approval. Here is exactly what happens.

  1. Load audit

    An engineer visits, lists every appliance you want to run and checks your roof, wiring and shading. Free in our branch cities.

  2. Design & quote

    You get a written quote with the exact inverter, panels and battery, backup hours and what it will save you.

  3. Installation

    Our own certified crew installs, tests and commissions the system, then shows your household how to use it.

  4. Aftercare

    Scheduled maintenance visits, warranty handling and a support line that answers when something beeps.
