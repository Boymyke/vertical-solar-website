How to care for your solar system

A few minutes of care keeps your panels producing, your inverter cool and your batteries healthy for years. Here is everything you can do yourself — and what to leave to us.

Report a fault
Safety first. Solar panels produce electricity whenever there is light, and batteries store a lot of energy. Never open the inverter, disconnect battery cables, or climb onto steep or high roofs. If you smell burning, see smoke or a swollen battery, switch off the inverter and battery breaker, keep everyone away and call us.

Solar panels

Clean off dust

Rinse with clean water and wipe gently with a soft brush or sponge on a pole. Clean in the cool morning or evening — never cold water on hot glass.

Every 2–4 weeks in harmattan

Watch for new shade

A growing tree, new water tank or neighbour's extension can shade panels. Even partial shade on one panel reduces the whole string's output.

Check every 3 months

Look, don't touch

From the ground, check for cracked glass, bird nests under panels, loose cables or damaged conduit. Report anything unusual.

Monthly

Avoid

Detergents, abrasive pads, pressure washers, standing or leaning on panels, and spraying water on the junction boxes or inverter.

Always

Inverter

Keep it cool and clear

Leave at least 30cm around the inverter. Never box it into a cupboard or place items on top. Heat shortens its life.

Always

Read the display

Learn the normal PV, load and battery readings on a sunny afternoon. If output drops on similar days, clean the panels or call us.

Weekly glance

Don't overload

Avoid starting the iron, microwave, pump and AC at the same time. Stagger heavy appliances to avoid overload alarms.

Daily habit

Keep pests out

Rats and insects damage cables. Keep the inverter room clean and dry, and seal cable entry points.

Monthly

Batteries

Lithium (LiFePO4)

  • No water top-ups needed — keep the area cool and dry.
  • Avoid running to 0%; let the inverter's low-battery cut-off do its job.
  • Keep the battery management system cables connected — they protect the cells.
  • If the battery shows a fault light, switch off its breaker and contact us.

Lead-acid / tubular

  • Check electrolyte levels monthly and top up with distilled water only.
  • Keep terminals clean and tight; a little petroleum jelly prevents corrosion.
  • Don't regularly discharge below 50% — it shortens battery life sharply.
  • Ensure the room is ventilated: these batteries release gas while charging.

Your seasonal checklist

Harmattan

  • Clean panels every two weeks
  • Expect cooler mornings and hazy output — that's normal
  • Check the inverter's air vents for dust

Rainy season

  • Confirm surge protection is in place before storms
  • Check cable conduits and roof penetrations for leaks
  • Set grid or generator charging for long cloudy spells
