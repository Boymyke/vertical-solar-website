Installation policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Before installation

  • An engineer completes a site survey and load audit.
  • You receive a written design and quote, and installation is scheduled after payment or deposit.
  • For rented properties, you confirm landlord consent.

On installation day

  • Our crew arrives with personal protective equipment and works under a site supervisor.
  • Power may be off for part of the day while we connect the system.
  • We keep the site tidy and remove packaging.

Standards

  • Correctly sized DC and AC cables, breakers, isolators and surge protection
  • Proper earthing and labelled distribution boards
  • Secure mounting suited to your roof type

Handover

We test and commission the system, show you how to read the inverter, and explain safe use. You receive warranty information, and your first maintenance date is booked in our system.

Changes and additions

Additional work outside the quote is agreed and priced in writing before it is carried out.
